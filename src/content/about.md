> *"We all like to think we're more complex than a two-sentence explanation, but a two-sentence explanation is usually what the world wants from us."*  -- Austin Kleon in his book 'Show your work!' (a good read, btw :D)

I am **Aditya**, a 24-year-old **regular dude**. I like to talk about various things, but most of my views and opinions are related to **technology** and **our future**. I did my Computer Engineering and then an MBA, and now I like to use all that I have learned (not a lot) to create stuff that **I** think is cool. 

By day, I work as a consultant at an IT company.

## What this place is

It's almost like a **personal diary** except that it is not personal because it is quite literally for everyone to see and it's nothing like a diary because this site will only contain curated thought-out ideas that I want to share with the world. 

Still, I will treat it like my personal daily diary... in the sense that I don't expect anyone else to read what I am writing. **The act of writing is for me to share more than for others to receive**. I will also  try to be consistent and write daily.


### Here is what each section means - 

- **Projects** will contain things that I am working on. Sometimes there might be a download link as well.
- **Writings** will be a place to find long form content written by me. You might find Essays, blog posts, reviews and more.
- **Tidbits** will contain short ideas or thoughts. Something like tweets.
- **Library**  will be where you can find stuff that I am currently consuming. TV Shows, Movies, Music, Books, Anime, Manga, etc. Sometimes you might find a note attached about the media.
